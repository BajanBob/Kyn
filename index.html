      Check your numbers now

      Why you need a KYN Health Passport

      Most serious long-term conditions start silently. In Barbados, about 4 in 10 adults have high blood pressure and nearly 1 in 5 has diabetes, and worldwide around 4 in 10 people with these conditions don't know it yet. One reading on one day tells you little. Readings over months show where you are heading, while there is still time to change course.

      Your numbers in one place

      Results from us, your doctor and your lab are usually scattered. The Passport keeps them together, in order, in your hands.

      Spot changes early

      We compare each reading with your last ones and tell you plainly if something is drifting, so you can act before it becomes a problem.

      Better doctor visits

      Bring a clear history instead of trying to remember. Your doctor sees trends at a glance and can make better decisions with you.

      Safer medicines

      An up-to-date medicines list helps every pharmacist and doctor avoid duplicates and harmful interactions.

      How it works

      1. JoinIn store, by WhatsApp or by calling 230-0322. Members and non-members of KYN Pharmacy are welcome.
      2. Starting checkWe take your blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, height and waist, and note your medicines and any lab results.
      3. Get your PassportYou receive your card and Passport number in standard, large-print or braille.
      4. Check and stampEach time you visit, we check again and stamp the new reading. We suggest at least every 3 months.
      5. Review your trendsA pharmacist explains what changed and what to do next, and you can share the summary with your doctor.
      6. Renew yearlyYour Passport runs for 12 months. Renew to keep your history and benefits going.
